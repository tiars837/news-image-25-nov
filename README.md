A one-page newspaper poster summing up the world news of Wednesday,
25 November 2026 — built for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125).

Lead story: the CIA and the U.S. spy chief's office were hit by a hack
of their unclassified open-source intelligence systems. Also inside:
Asian markets slide as the AI rally fades, Pakistan's first astronaut
reaches orbit aboard Shenzhou-24, 30 hostages seized in a Cartagena
supermarket, WHO pulls Ebola staff out of a hospital in DR Congo, and
Russia calls Ukraine's drone strikes on its ships "terrorism".

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to `/output`, or to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set. Python 3.13 standard library only,
no network, fully deterministic — two runs produce identical bytes.
